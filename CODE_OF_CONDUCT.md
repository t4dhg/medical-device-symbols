# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Keep participation respectful and constructive. Discuss technical decisions candidly, but direct technical criticism at the work rather than the person. Protect the privacy of contributors and users, including information shared in reports, examples, and logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers enforce this policy in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. They may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and protect the people affected.

Privately report conduct concerns through [the repository's private report form](https://github.com/t4dhg/medical-device-symbols/security/advisories/new). Start the report title with “Code of Conduct” and do not post sensitive details in a public issue or discussion. Maintainers will keep the report as private as reasonably possible while reviewing and addressing it.
